## Users

## Product Purpose

Deliver an aesthetic, reassuring "safe-space" dental clinic portal for Smile Today Dental Clinic - Zamboanga City Branch, providing procedure discovery, doctor credentials, painless Swiss Airflow technology, and online appointment scheduling.

## Positioning

A gentle, high-trust dental clinic in Canelar, Zamboanga City that eliminates dental fear and anxiety through friendly consultation, modern technology, and clear pricing.

## Operating Context

- **Patients**: Accessing primarily from mobile devices and WhatsApp/Viber channels in Zamboanga City, seeking pain-free care, aesthetic smile transformations, or emergency toothache relief.
- **Staff**: Operating from the clinic reception desk on Mayor Jaldon St., triaging calls and bookings.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Patient Experience**: Comprehensive service catalog (Airflow prophylaxis, braces, aligners, odontectomy, veneers, fillings), Dr. Shaira U. Tillah bio, fast appointment booking, instant Viber/call triggers, and precise local landmark directions.
- **Languages**: English, Filipino (Tagalog), and local Chavacano greetings.

## Brand Commitments

- "Safe-space" calming aesthetic that eliminates dental fear.
